## What 横折弯钩 is

The most complex compound stroke we'll teach: heng → corner-down → curve-right → up-hook. The signature stroke of 力, 万, 几, 风, 凡, 飞, 九, ...

## Form (4 segments — most complex so far)

- **Seg A (short heng):** (-80,+120) → (+80,+120). w 16→11→14.
- **Seg B (vertical drop):** (+80,+120) → (+80,-60). w 14→11.
- **Seg C (quarter-curve right):** (+80,-60) → (+140,-100). Concave-up arc. w 11→12.
- **Seg D (up-hook):** (+140,-100) → (+170,-50). w 12→3 (taper).

This composes 横折 (A+B from c7) + 竖弯钩's curve+hook (C+D from c10) in one continuous stroke.
