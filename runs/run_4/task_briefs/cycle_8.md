# Cycle 8 — Focus: 竖钩 (shu_gou, vertical with leftward hook)

Phase 2 compound. Single-phase (eval=vision). Self-preview 2.

## What 竖钩 is

A vertical stroke that ends with a small upward-left hook (钩) at the bottom. Used as the central stroke of 寸, 守, 子, 字, 小, 才, 水, 永, ...

## Canonical form

Two segments per §1.5:
- **Seg A (shu arm):** top (0, +180) → bottom (0, -150). Width 16 (top entry) → 11 (shaft) → 14 (pre-hook 顿笔).
- **Seg B (hook arm):** from (0, -150) sweeping up-left to (-60, -110). Short ~70 px diagonal. Width 14 → 3 (taper to fine point).

The hook is the visual signature. Without it, this reads as 竖 (the c2 entry).
