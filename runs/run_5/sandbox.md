## Generalizable finding (draft, not yet promoted)

A **width profile that bells in the middle** (light–heavy–light) is wrong for 楷书 横. The correct profile is **entry-press → moderate shaft → CLOSING-press at the right end** (right end heaviest). This is the 起笔/行笔/收笔 pattern in calligraphy. If the right end of a 横 is the thinnest point of the stroke, the 顿笔 criterion is failed.

If the Drawer's next attempt fixes this, promote the rule to Principle Bank §1.1.
