# Teaching Plan — run_6

## Mission

Teach the Drawer to produce Chinese characters that are structurally
faithful to MMH and visually unambiguous. The artifact under study is
the **memory content** (Success Bank + Principle Bank + to_be_learned).
Character pass rate is the *measure* of memory quality.

## Phases

| Phase | Cycles | Focus | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | c1–c6 | 6 atomic strokes: 横, 竖, 撇, 捺, 提, 点 | anchor placement |
| 1.5 | c7–c13 | 7 compound strokes: 横折, 竖钩, 横折钩, 竖弯钩, 横撇, 竖折, 横折弯钩 | anchor placement + corner |
| 2 | c14+ | simple characters whose strokes are all in the bank | full 5-gate |
| 3 | later | multi-component characters reusing mastered sub-characters | full 5-gate + reuse |

## Mastery rule

A focus is mastered only when `structural_pass` AND `judge_panel.unanimous_yes` both hold. No exceptions. Carry-over = next cycle, same focus.
