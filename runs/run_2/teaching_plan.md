# Teaching plan

## Current phase

**Phase 1 — atomic strokes** (cold start, cycle 1). The Drawer is a
fresh subagent with an empty memory. We begin from zero by design.

## Pedagogy — depth over breadth, no skipping

The hard gate: I may not leave Phase 1 while any stroke I have
introduced is still below the fidelity threshold `visual_score >= 0.85`
on a post-reflection cycle. The composite judge rewards the deliberate
calligraphic detail in the GTs (顿笔 pauses, 小折 small folds, 弧度
curvature), so 0.85 means "the detail is actually there", not just
"topologically close". I will drill a stroke until it clears the bar
rather than racing to cover more.

Order of the curriculum:
1. The **6 atomic strokes** first — 点 横 竖 撇 捺 提. Everything else
   is built from these; they must be mastered before any compound
   stroke or character.
2. Then **compound strokes**, grouped by family (heng-folds,
   shu-folds, hooks/curves), introduced only after their atomic
   constituents are mastered.
3. Only after the introduced stroke set is mastered may Phase 2
   (characters) begin.

Batch size is 6. Cycle 1 = exactly the 6 atomic strokes, so the very
first batch establishes the foundation in one pass and the carry-over
rule then drills whichever ones fail.

## OCR policy

Phase 1 strokes are not characters — OCR is noise. Dataset
`judge.use_ocr = false` for all stroke cycles. The composite visual
fidelity score is the only signal here.
