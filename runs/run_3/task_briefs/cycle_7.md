# Cycle 7 — Task brief (carry-over + 撇+捺 family expansion)

This batch carries the two unmastered characters from c6 (大, 入) plus
four new characters that exercise the same 撇/捺 composition family
(又, 个, 不, 木). The same numeric fixes from `drawer_memory.md`
apply broadly across this batch.

## Judgment

Eval: **gt+ocr+vision**. Pass = `is_correct == true` AND
`calligraphy_rubric.total >= 7` (no 0 criterion).

## Repair targets (carry-overs)

1. **大 — heng length too short last time.** The heng must extend
   well past the 撇/捺 crossing points (target: heng length ≈ 1.4–1.6×
   the horizontal distance between the limbs at the crossing height).
   Apex of 撇/捺 well above the heng. Heng cuts through both limbs at
   ~30–40% down from apex.
2. **入 — junction too high last time.** The 捺 must start on the
   撇's spine at **45–55%** down from the head (c6 was ~35% → still
   read as 人). The right extent of 入 is dominated by the 捺.

## New compositions

3. **又 (2 strokes):** 横撇 (a compound stroke — starts upper-left as
   a short heng, then turns 45° down to become a 撇 tail) + 捺
   crossing through. The 横撇 looks like a hook turning into a
   downstroke; the 捺 sweeps from upper-middle through to lower-right.
4. **个 (3 strokes):** 撇 + 捺 sharing top apex (like 人), then a
   shu dropping straight down from the apex through the middle. The
   shu is the third stroke, vertical, centered.
5. **不 (4 strokes):** heng (top) + 撇 (long, head on the heng's
   left half, sweeping down-left) + shu (centered, below the heng) +
   点 (right of the shu, midway down).
6. **木 (4 strokes):** heng + shu (centered, crossing through) + 撇
   (head at heng-shu intersection, sweeping down-left) + 捺 (head
   at the same intersection, sweeping down-right).

## Required calligraphic detail

Brushed sweep with per-sample pensize on every stroke (middle ≥ 50%
of peak). Use the heavy-end cheat sheet keyed to stroke identity.
For compound strokes (又's 横撇), draw as one continuous brushed path
with a 顿笔 thickening at the turn.

Save each PNG as `attempts/cycle_7/<idx>_<char>.png` (character glyph
in the filename).
